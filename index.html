    研究方法 — 课堂笔记

    Strathmore Business School · MBA

    第一节课

    2026-08-17

    1. Why this course matters（这门课为什么重要）

    More than a unit on your transcript.（不只是成绩单上的一门课）

    • Every MBA candidate must produce a project, dissertation or thesis to graduate.
      每个 MBA 学生都必须完成一份 project / dissertation / thesis 才能毕业。
    • This course builds the exact skills your dissertation will demand — topic, problem, literature, design, ethics, analysis, writing.
      这门课练的正是写论文要用的技能：选题、问题、文献、设计、伦理、分析、写作。
    • The Chapter 1 you draft here becomes the real Chapter 1 of your dissertation.
      你在这门课里写的第一章，就是论文真正的第一章。
    • Skip it or push it now, and you relearn it later — under supervision, on the clock, with less room to fail.
      现在跳过或拖延，以后还得重学一遍——那时有导师盯着、有时间压力、犯错空间更小。

    2. The real stakes（真正的利害所在）

    What happens after this course?（这门课之后会发生什么？）

    • Passing this course properly is your ticket to supervisor allocation.
      把这门课真正学好（而不是勉强及格），才是拿到导师分配的门票。
    • Strathmore allocates supervisors within one month of completing Research Methods.
      Strathmore 在 Research Methods 结课后一个月内分配导师。
    • Struggle here, delay your Chapter 1, and that clock simply does not start.
      这门课学不动、第一章拖着，那个一个月的计时根本不会开始。
    No solid research methods pass → No supervisor → No dissertation progress
    没有扎实通过研究方法 → 没有导师 → 论文毫无进展

    3. Before we begin — Ground Rules（课堂规则）

    • Be present and on time — 5:30pm sharp, every session.
      每次课 5:30pm 准时到，不迟到。
    • Come with your study interest — we work on real ideas, not hypotheticals.
      带着自己的研究兴趣来，课上做真实的想法，不做假设性练习。
    • Come with your PC always, do personal reading and practices.
      每次都带电脑；课外自己阅读和练习。
    • Deadlines are fixed — the CAT and concept-paper dates do not move.
      截止日期不改：CAT 和 concept paper 的日期不会往后挪。
    • Full participation — syndicate work is graded engagement, not attendance.
      小组作业算的是参与质量的分，不是点名出勤。
    • Zero tolerance for plagiarism, ethical use of AI from your very first paragraph.
      抄袭零容忍；从第一段起就要合规使用 AI。
    • Respect the room and FOCUS — computers open when required, phones down — only when necessary, full professional conduct.
      尊重课堂、保持专注：需要时才开电脑，手机收起来，全程专业举止。

    4. Set your expectations（把预期定好）

    Challenges students commonly face（学生普遍会遇到的困难）

    • Choosing one feasible topic, not ten exciting ones.
      选一个做得成的题，而不是十个听起来很带感的题。
    • Balancing full-time work against research demands.
      全职工作与研究投入之间的平衡。
    • Weak literature-search and academic-writing habits.
      文献检索和学术写作的习惯薄弱。
    • Uncertainty over which methodology and design to choose.
      不确定该选哪种方法论和研究设计。
    • Getting real access to organisations and respondents.
      真正拿到企业和受访者的调研入口。
    • Delays in obtaining ethical clearance.
      伦理审批（ethical clearance）下来得慢。

    5. The cost of falling behind（落后的代价）

    Setbacks of not completing on time（没按时完成的后果）

    • Extensions require fees, paperwork and dean-level approval.
      申请延期要交费、走流程，还要院长级别审批。
    • A maximum study period is enforced — exceed it, and you are de-registered.
      最长学习年限是硬性的——超过就被注销学籍。
    • Missed course cohorts mean missed graduation dates.
      错过一届的课，就错过对应的毕业时间。
    Time lost at this stage is rarely recovered later in the programme.
    这个阶段丢掉的时间，在后面的课程里几乎补不回来。

    Read this twice — Why a noble idea still fails（好想法为什么还是会挂）

    • A brilliant idea with no feasible design will not survive proposal defence.
      想法再好，设计不可行，proposal 答辩过不去。
    • Data collected before ethical clearance = automatic disqualification. No exceptions.
      在伦理审批下来之前就收数据 = 直接取消资格，没有例外。
    • Objectives that don't match your problem statement get sent back for rewrite.
      研究目标和问题陈述对不上，就打回重写。
    • Your entire study needs to be in sync — from the topic to the recommendations.
      整篇研究要前后一致：从选题一路到最后的建议。
    • Formatting and structure that ignore the SU guide will not be accepted for examination.
      格式和结构不按 SU（Strathmore University）指南来，不予送审。
    Rigour — not novelty / innovation — is what carries you through to graduation.
    严谨（rigour）才能把你送到毕业，不是新颖或创新。

    6. Grounding the study area（把研究领域落地）

    What is business & management research?（什么是商业与管理研究？）

    • Research is systematic enquiry that increases knowledge（Saunders, Lewis & Thornhill, Ch. 1）
      研究是系统性的探究，其目的是增加知识。
    • It is deliberate investigation — distinct from opinion, assumption or anecdote.
      研究是有意识、有计划的调查——区别于观点、假设和道听途说。
    • Business research applies this same discipline to solve real organisational problems.
      商业研究把同样的严谨方法用来解决真实的组织问题。

    7. The Road Ahead（接下来的路）

    The research process, start to finish（研究的完整流程）

    1. Formulate Topic — 确定选题
    2. Review Literature — 文献综述
    3. Design the Study — 研究设计
    4. Collect Data — 收集数据
    5. Analyse Data — 分析数据
    6. Write & Present — 写作与呈现
    Every stage matters — a weak start (topic, philosophy) undermines everything after it.
    每一步都算数——开头（选题、研究哲学）不牢，后面全被拖累。

    课上参考图：教材里的完整流程

    对应 Saunders, Lewis & Thornhill (2022), Figure 1.2 The research process, p.13 —— 括号内为教材章节号。

    Begin to think about research开始思考研究
    ↓
    Generate your research idea and develop the proposal (Ch. 2)形成研究想法、写出 proposal
    ↓
    Critically review the literature (Ch. 3)批判性文献综述
    ↓
    Understand your philosophy and approach (Ch. 4)明确研究哲学与研究路径
    ↓
    Formulate your research design (Ch. 5)确定研究设计
    ↓
    Negotiate access and address ethical issues (Ch. 6)争取调研入口、处理伦理问题
    ↓
    Plan your data collection and collect data considering one or more of:规划并收集数据，从以下方式中选一种或多种
    Sampling(Ch. 7)抽样
    Secondary data(Ch. 8)二手数据
    Observation(Ch. 9)观察
    Interviews and research diaries(Ch. 10)访谈与研究日志
    Questionnaires(Ch. 11)问卷
    ↓
    Analyse your data either or both:分析数据，二者之一或两者兼用
    Quantitatively(Ch. 12)定量
    Qualitatively(Ch. 13)定性
    ↓
    Write your project report and prepare your presentation (Ch. 14)撰写研究报告、准备答辩
    ↓
    Submit your project report and give your presentation提交报告并完成答辩
    图中两种箭头：实线 = Forward planning（向前推进）；虚线 = Reflection and revision（回看与修改）—— 每个阶段都可以往回连到任一前序阶段，所以流程是迭代的，不是一条直线。左侧贯穿全程的竖条是 Writing and reflecting：写作和反思从第一天做到最后一天，不是最后才开始。
